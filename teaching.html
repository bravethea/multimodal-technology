Teaching

The group teaches at the Department of Computational Linguistics and in the excellence programme of the Digital Society Initiative, and runs workshops on computational and quantitative methods for language and multimodal data. Workshop materials are released openly where the course design allows it.

Theses

The group supervises Bachelor's and Master's theses on multimodal analysis, speech and video processing, and corpus construction. Students with a concrete interest in one of the research topics are welcome to write with a short description of what they would like to work on.
