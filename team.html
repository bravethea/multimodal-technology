Team

The group brings together researchers working on speech and language processing, computer vision, corpus linguistics and research infrastructure. Doctoral projects are supervised within the Department of Computational Linguistics and, where a project is interdisciplinary, jointly with partner groups.

Group leader

  • Dr. Teodora Vuković Group leader and principal investigator

    Computational analysis of multimodal human communication, multimodal corpus infrastructure, and processing of spoken and low-resource language data. She leads CAPIRE, the DAVA Lab, the MAVA Cluster and MOSAIC, and co-leads the LiRI Corpus Platform. PhD in low-resource natural language processing, University of Zurich, 2022.

Doctoral researchers

  • Masoumeh Chapariniya Doctoral researcher, 2023–2027

    Multimodal personal identity recognition. Her work examines whether individuals can be identified from conversational dynamics — movement, timing and interactional behaviour — rather than from appearance alone.
