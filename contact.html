Collaboration

The group collaborates with research groups, infrastructures and companies that work with audio-visual, behavioural or physiological data. Enquiries about joint projects, data analysis or the use of the group's software are welcome by e-mail.

For questions concerning the LiRI Corpus Platform and other infrastructure services, please also see the pages of the Linguistic Research Infrastructure.

Doctoral and Master's projects

Prospective doctoral researchers should write to the group leader with a short description of the research question they would like to pursue, a curriculum vitae and a transcript of records. Proposals that connect to one of the current research topics can be discussed before a formal application is submitted. Doctoral admission is decided by the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences; the group can advise on the procedure but does not admit candidates itself.

Students at the University of Zurich who would like to write a Bachelor's or Master's thesis in the group are welcome to get in touch at any point in the semester. Topics are usually developed from an ongoing project, so that the work contributes to a dataset, a tool or an evaluation that is already under way.
