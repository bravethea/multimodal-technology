Resources and software

The group releases its corpora, annotation tools and processing pipelines where licensing and data protection allow it. Datasets are deposited in repositories that issue persistent identifiers; software is published under open licences.

Platforms and software

2025

LiRI Corpus Platform

A web-based infrastructure for building, querying and analysing corpora that combine transcribed speech, video and multiple annotation layers. Developed at the Linguistic Research Infrastructure, University of Zurich. [link]

2023

VIAN-DH

Software for multimodal video analysis, developed for research in the digital humanities and since integrated into the LiRI Corpus Platform.

2019

Part-of-speech tagger for spoken Torlak

A tagger trained on the spoken Torlak corpus, released as Torlak ReLDI Tagger. [link]

Corpora and datasets

in preparation

Spoken Torlak dialect corpus 2.0 and part-of-speech tagger

An extended version of the corpus with revised transcription and annotation, and a retrained tagger. Expected 2026.

2020

Spoken Torlak dialect corpus 1.0

Transcribed recordings of the Timok variety of Torlak, collected in fieldwork in eastern Serbia between 2015 and 2018. Deposited with CLARIN.SI and queryable through NoSketch Engine and KonText. [link]

2017

DIWNA corpus of oral narratives

A corpus of transcribed and annotated oral narratives, created in the Constructing Narratives project with Humboldt University Berlin and the Institute for Balkan Studies, Belgrade.

2017

ReLDI-NormTag-sr 1.0

A Serbian training corpus of social media text, annotated for normalisation and tagging. Deposited with CLARIN.SI. [link]

2015

Bunjevac dialect corpus and dialect dictionaries

A corpus of the Bunjevac dialect, with online dictionaries of the Bunjevac and Šokac dialects, created at the Institute for Balkan Studies, Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts.

Using these resources

Corpora that contain identifiable speech or video are released under access conditions set by the consent given by the recorded participants. Where a dataset cannot be published openly, the group documents its structure and provides access on request for research purposes.

If you use one of these resources, please cite the accompanying publication listed on the publications page.
